JASPERCRAFT OBSIDIAN RULE - 2026-10-01

Obsidian cannot be destroyed/replaced by mob block-change events or any entity
or block explosion. Fire events also leave it intact. The global listener runs
even when automatic disasters are disabled. No timers/world scans or changes
to damage, creature spawning, lighting, player mining or other materials.

Hellstorm craters, netherrack floors and meteor/hellstorm magma scatter explicitly
exclude obsidian. Ordinary fire above obsidian may still exist; this protects
the block, not players from disaster damage. Invasion miners hard-reject obsidian
even if an administrator removes it from their configurable unbreakable list.
Apocalypse's existing SiegeRules already excludes obsidian for digs and TNT
carriers, and that plugin/source is not rebuilt or changed.

JasprDisasters 1.2.1 and JasprInvasions 1.1.1 are built from the exact current
deployed baseline. All unchanged deployed payloads are retained. Three existing
source/binary differences in DisasterPlugin inner classes and ThunderHellStorm
are preserved rather than overwritten by a whole stale rebuild. See private
reports/build.json. Keep this preservation step in any subsequent rebuild.
Intentional player-operated puzzle/portal doors remain unchanged.

Build candidates privately using scripts/build.cjs; do not rerun against changed
production without taking a new baseline. prepare-release.cjs verifies payload

server and a five-minute restart gap, backs up source/JARs, stages only the two
JARs, then requests one clean restart. No maintenance-mode or world rewrites.

The rule covers the current mob destruction APIs and audited direct mutation
paths. A future plugin that calls Block.setType directly must preserve obsidian
at that call site; Bukkit does not emit grief/explosion events for such edits.
Exact deployment backup, live verification and recoverable checkpoint are
recorded in build/release/ and reports/ in the isolated workspace.
